# Privacy notice: Finance Tracker (personal)

Finance Tracker is a private, non-commercial tool used by one household. It is not offered to anyone else.

**Who is responsible:** the private individual who registered this application with Enable Banking Oy. Contact: the data protection email given in that registration.

**What data:** account information the user chooses to share from their own bank accounts through Enable Banking Oy, a licensed account information service provider. That is account names, balances and transactions (date, amount, text, counterparty).

**Why:** to give the user's own household an overview of its finances. Nothing else: no marketing, no profiling, no sale or sharing of data.

**Where it is kept:** on the user's own home server in Sweden, reachable only by the user. It is not sent to any other service.

**How long:** for as long as the tool is in use. It can be deleted at any time.

**Who else sees it:** no one. Enable Banking and the bank handle the connection under their own terms.

**Consent:** access is read-only, lasts at most 180 days, and can be withdrawn at any time in the bank or in the Enable Banking Control Panel.

Last updated: 28 October 2026.
