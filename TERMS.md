# Terms of use: Finance Tracker (personal)

1. Finance Tracker is a private tool for personal, non-commercial use by a single household. It is not a service offered to the public, and no one else can sign up.
2. It only reads account information (balances and transactions) from accounts that belong to its user, through Enable Banking Oy. It never initiates payments.
3. Access to a bank account lasts at most 180 days and can be withdrawn at any time in the bank or in the Enable Banking Control Panel.
4. The tool is provided as is, without any warranty.
5. Contact: the data protection email given in the application's registration with Enable Banking.
